INSTITUTIONAL TERMS OF SERVICE

Terms and Conditions

Effective Date: 17 October 2026 • Directorate of Academic Affairs

1. Institutional Agreement

These Terms of Service govern the use of the Swami Rama Himalayan University Attendance and Academic Scheduling Portal by enrolled students, appointed faculty, and authorized administrative staff. By accessing this platform, users agree to uphold university academic integrity standards.

2. Academic Attendance Requirements

Pursuant to university academic ordinances, enrolled students must maintain a minimum attendance of seventy-five percent (75%) in every registered theoretical and laboratory course to qualify for end-semester examinations. The portal provides official calculations that are authoritative for determining examination admit card eligibility.

3. Acceptable Use & Accountability

  • Users must access the portal exclusively using their assigned institutional credentials and maintain strict password confidentiality.
  • Sharing account credentials, fabricating attendance submissions, or attempting unauthorized privilege elevation constitutes academic misconduct and will be referred to the College Disciplinary Committee.
  • Faculty members are obligated to file session registers within twenty-four (24) hours of conducting instructional sessions to maintain up-to-date institutional telemetry.

4. Register Discrepancies and Audit Corrections

Submitted attendance registers cannot be silently modified. Any legitimate correction arising from administrative or technical errors must follow the verified institutional reconciliation protocol, requiring departmental head endorsement and permanent audit logging.

5. Service Availability

While Swami Rama Himalayan University strives for continuous portal availability, planned maintenance windows are scheduled during off-peak hours and announced via the institutional calendar. Unscheduled interruptions will not alter academic deadlines without written notification from the Registrar.

6. Institutional Contacts

For technical inquiries or policy clarification, please contact the Directorate of Academic Information Systems at registrar-academic@apexcollege.edu.
