How it works

The portal provides simple attendance tracking for students and teachers.

Everyone

Sign in with your assigned ID and password. The application functions on mobile phones, tablets, and desktop browsers.

Students

Check your attendance percentage for each enrolled subject. View session records and check whether you meet the 75% requirement.

Teachers

Take attendance for assigned classes, create continuous evaluation assignments, and record sessional examination marks.

Examinations

Admins and teachers manage assignments, sessionals, and final semester marks with bulk Excel imports and audited result publication.

About

College attendance management

The Attendance Portal is a college-operated system designed to provide consistent attendance records and schedule management.

The platform ensures students have direct visibility into their attendance records, faculty can mark registers efficiently, and administrative departments can maintain compliance with institutional policies.
