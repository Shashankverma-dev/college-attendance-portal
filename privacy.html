DATA GOVERNANCE & PRIVACY NOTICE

Institutional Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 17 October 2026 • Directorate of Academic Information Systems

1. Institutional Authority & Scope

Swami Rama Himalayan University operates the Attendance Portal as part of its official student information and academic compliance infrastructure. This policy describes how student, faculty, and administrative attendance records, access logs, and related credentials are collected, managed, and safeguarded.

2. Categories of Information Collected

  • Authentication Records: Student registration numbers, faculty identification numbers (FIN), cryptographic password hashes, and session authentication tokens.
  • Attendance Sessions: Session timestamps, course code, section identifiers, scheduled slot hours, and faculty submission records.
  • Individual Attendance Statuses: Present, unexcused absence, or approved institutional/medical leave for each enrolled student per conducted session.
  • Audit Telemetry: Record creation and revision timestamps, approving authority identifiers, and attendance condonation documentation.

3. Institutional Purpose of Data Use

Attendance information is gathered strictly for academic governance, including verifying course completion, calculating eligibility for semester end-term examinations under the 75% statutory rule, notifying students of potential shortages, and fulfilling state and national accreditation reporting obligations.

4. Access Control and Role Segregation

In accordance with strict principle-of-least-privilege guidelines:

  • Students possess read-only access strictly confined to their own enrolled courses, session history, and percentage calculations. No student may access records of peers.
  • Faculty Members possess access to assigned course rosters to record and verify class registers during active terms.
  • Academic Deans & Heads of Department review department-level aggregate summaries, unsubmitted register alerts, and approved medical leaves.

5. Retention and Archive Policies

Official attendance registers are permanently retained throughout a student's active degree matriculation and archived for seven (7) academic years post-graduation pursuant to university statutory records retention regulations.

6. Rectification & Data Corrections

Inquiries regarding attendance discrepancies, medical leaves, or audit verification must be addressed to the Office of the Academic Dean at registrar-academic@apexcollege.edu.
